Safe native reuse and limitations
--------------------------------
The archived probe is NEVER imported, executed, or patched. Its global PROJECT
and OUT therefore cannot redirect imports or write to a historical session path.
Instead the adapter directly calls unchanged evaluation.grid_relief APIs:
  _market_config("nyiso_nyc", explicit_runtime_directory, mode)
  _run_episode(..., config_path=..., trace_dir=..., simulation_mode=mode)
  _metrics(agent_frame, reference_frame)

The original trace-reader and all component-summary assignment ASTs are checked
for exact equality against the byte-verified archived probe. This retains the
original pandas default CSV reading, top-decile masks, signed battery term,
power-component arithmetic, observation/commitment fields and mode-specific
native metric validation. No source function is monkeypatched.

Important inherited behavior: native legacy_v0 _run_episode itself calls its
original _align_calendar and _bias_regd hook. That existing hook replaces the
grid instance's RegD method as part of the archived legacy semantics. The adapter
does not add or modify it. Removing it would change the experiment. If "no
monkeypatching" is intended to prohibit even this unchanged native internal
legacy behavior, exact legacy replay is impossible under that stronger reading;
do not replace it with a different bias/physics implementation.

Validation semantics
--------------------
These are 24-hour service-only diagnostic pairs. Each native trajectory must
complete 17280 hardware steps and 96 macro steps, truncate normally, and not
terminate. The unchanged native _metrics qualification and physical-mode paired
exogenous equality checks remain in force. There is NO two-hour recovery and no
invented 26-hour tracking SOC/backlog/90%-service-floor qualification.

Every reported per-case component is compared, not just mean relief. Floats use
atol=1e-9, rtol=1e-9; identities, counts, flags and missing/extra fields are exact.
Three power balances are independently checked with absolute tolerance 1e-8 MW:
  relief = cooling relief + other-facility relief
  relief = nominal facility - actual facility
  relief = demanded response - signed battery discharge + true metered error
Negative battery discharge means charging; it must not be clipped to zero.

Do not treat the modes as identical realized exogenous inputs merely because
day/seed labels match. Their calendar, workload, weather and signal semantics
are intentionally those of the original diagnostic. The results are observed
power-balance reproduction, not individual-change causal attribution.
